- **The Enforcement Trigger:** CMS began quarterly random Section 111 compliance audits in **January 2026**, with initial Informal Notices sent in March 2026 (Liability/No-Fault) and July 2026 (Workers' Compensation).

- **RRE Direct Liability:** Penalties attach strictly to the self-insured entity (RRE), NOT the reporting agent or third-party administrator (TPA).
